How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, search for “Christmas” and pick a size.

How do I cancel Christmas Premium?

On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Christmas Countdown. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. To restore a purchase, open Premium in the app and tap Restore.

Where is my data stored?

Everything (Secret Santa names, gift lists, opened Advent doors, your photo) is stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes it.

The countdown shows the wrong date

The countdown uses your iPhone's date and time zone. You can switch to Orthodox Christmas (January 7) in Settings inside the app.
